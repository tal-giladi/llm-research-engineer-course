| Gumbel substitution: removing sampler drift from RL without trusting the sampler | https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27375 (verified; 20 Nov 2026) | Coordinator fixes public per-position Gumbel noise; trainer recomputes argmax(logits_p + G) and scores that token, so the drift term from training-inference mismatch vanishes without needing the sampler's q(y); 9B model trained on a permissionless network for 158 steps (authors).
