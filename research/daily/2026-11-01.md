### C-20261101-01 · WISD: Weakening-Induced On-Policy Self-Distillation for Large Reasoning Models

- **Class:** B
- **Date discovered:** 2026-11-01
- **Date published:** 2026-10-28 (v1)
- **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38681 (verified resolves; v1, Wed 28 Oct 2026 18:55:38 UTC, cs.AI primary, cross-listed cs.CL/cs.LG)
- **Organization/researchers:** Yecheng Wu, Song Han, Han Cai. Song Han and Han Cai are established MIT efficient-ML researchers (MIT HAN Lab — AWQ, SmoothQuant, TinyML and related work), general knowledge, not independently re-confirmed as to institutional affiliation on the abstract page itself (the page states no affiliations).
- **Category:** RL/post-training (on-policy self-distillation without an external or privileged teacher)
- **What changed:** The course's accepted lesson 17.4 (`lessons/module-17/lesson-04.md`) and its actively-tracked deferred cluster `research/deferred/on-policy-distillation-variants.md` (10 prior candidates) cover on-policy distillation variants that rely on either an external teacher or a "privileged" version of the same model (extra context/ground truth at training time) — and one already-tracked paper in that cluster (arXiv 2609.36642, PR-OPD) specifically explains *why* privileged self-distillation works via hidden-state alignment. WISD proposes the opposite construction: instead of strengthening the teacher side, it *weakens* a copy of the same model (via post-training-free low-bit quantization or magnitude pruning, no retraining) and uses the gap between the original and the weakened copy as the on-policy training signal — removing the need for any privileged information, external teacher, or extra ground-truth context.
- **Technical summary:** Authors' own results across several reasoning-model architectures and scales: weakening via quantization improves average accuracy by 4.0 percentage points and via pruning by 5.5 points over the base model on math/coding reasoning benchmarks; a concrete worked example given is pruning raising Qwen3.5-4B from 57.7% to 70.5% on HMMT 2025 and from 39.1% to 56.7% on LiveCodeBench v6. Code is released and independently confirmed to exist and resolve: https://github.com/jet-ai-projects/WISD (public, Apache-2.0, README documents a full data-curation/train/eval pipeline with shell scripts, a single-node 8-GPU reference recipe and a CPU pytest suite; not independently re-run in this check). No independent third-party reproduction found; no stated venue (marked "work in progress" in the arXiv comments field).
- **Why it might matter:** A mechanistically distinct, code-backed variant within the course's already-active OPD-variants cluster: it sidesteps the privileged-information requirement that several other tracked variants (including the already-flagged PR-OPD critique) treat as central to why self-distillation works at all, by manufacturing the needed capability gap through weight-space degradation (quantization/pruning) instead. If it replicates, it is a concrete counterexample to "self-distillation needs privileged context."
- **Evidence of adoption:** None found — single paper, authors' own benchmark results, no third-party reproduction, no stated venue, three days old at discovery. Code release and credible author affiliation (MIT HAN Lab researchers) are evidence of care, not adoption.
- **Major organizations using it:** None identified as adopters; MIT-affiliated authors (per general knowledge of the named researchers, not stated on the page) are the originators.
- **Open-source implementation:** https://github.com/jet-ai-projects/WISD (verified: public repo, Apache-2.0, README with runnable-looking pipeline).
- **Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38681
- **Code:** https://github.com/jet-ai-projects/WISD
- **Relationship to existing course material:** Direct extension of the WAIT topic `research/deferred/on-policy-distillation-variants.md` (next review 2026-11-29) and adjacent to the accepted `research/accepted/on-policy-distillation.md` (lesson 17.4). Checked via `lookup "on-policy distillation"` (hits exactly these two files plus `lessons/module-17/lesson-04.md`/`code/src/llmre/reasoning/distill.py`, confirming this is the same topic family, not a duplicate requiring no record) — new evidence (a distinct no-privileged-information construction), not a restatement of any of the ten already-tracked candidates in that file.
