# Inherited, not invented: provenance of the 'wait' self-correction marker in reasoning models

## Summary

Zero-RL rarely introduces the 'wait' marker into base models lacking it; it enters via training data (mid-training, SFT on traces). Marker frequency alone is not evidence of RL-driven reasoning change; LLM-judge revision metrics can reverse human-labelled trends.
