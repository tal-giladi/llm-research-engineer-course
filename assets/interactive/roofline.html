Where does this kernel sit on the roofline?

Pick a GPU and a kernel, move the sliders, and watch the dot. Left of the dashed ridge the kernel is memory-bound (only fewer bytes help); right of it, compute-bound (only fewer or faster FLOPs help).

Model: time ≈ max(FLOPs ÷ peak, bytes ÷ bandwidth). Bytes count only HBM traffic, each tensor read or written once per kernel; real kernels add launch overhead, caches and imperfect overlap, so measured numbers land below the roof. Attention is one head, fp16, FLOPs ≈ 4·T²·d (the two matmuls; the softmax adds a few FLOPs per score).
